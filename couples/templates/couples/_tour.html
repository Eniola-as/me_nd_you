Welcome to Me & You

This is your private couple space. To play together, send your partner an invitation link from here, or paste the one they sent you. You'll always see who invited you before you join.

In Questions and Spicy Only, each of you answers privately. Your answers are revealed together once you've both answered. If your partner would lose an answer, Next asks first.

Chat from the bubble in the corner. Your messages say Sent once they're saved, and Seen once your partner has read them.

Notifications tell you when your partner messages you, answers or wants to switch games. They only say what happened, never what anyone wrote. You can turn them on for this device below.

You can be in one couple space at a time. Joining another one closes your current space for both of you, so you'll always be asked to confirm first.
