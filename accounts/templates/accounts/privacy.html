Version: {{ policy_version }}

Me & You is in beta. It's a free, early version: features may change, and it may sometimes be unavailable or lose live updates (see "Beta limits" below).

Me & You is run by Adewale Saheed, based in Nigeria. Contact: {{ contact_email }}. We reply within 30 days.

Playing safely

  • Me & You is for adults aged 18 or older.
  • Prompts and dares are suggestions, not obligations. Either partner can skip a prompt or stop playing at any time.
  • Me & You is a game, not relationship, medical or psychological advice.

What we collect

  • Your account: email address, a password (stored only as a one-way hash we can't read), your display name, when you joined and last signed in, and whether you verified your email.
  • Your confirmations: that you're 18 or older, and the consents you gave at sign-up, with the date and version of this notice.
  • Your couple space: who's in it, invitation links (stored only as a fingerprint, and they expire), and the custom questions you and your partner add, with who wrote each one (from the beta on).
  • Games: the current card, the previous card's text and game progress. Answers in Questions and Spicy Only are kept only until either of you moves to the next question or resets the game, and whether each of you has seen the revealed answers.
  • Chat: your messages and reactions, when you were last seen, and how far you've read (your partner sees this as "Seen" on their messages).
  • Notifications: what kind of thing happened (for example, "sent you a message") and when, never what anyone wrote. If you turn on notifications on a device, that device's push address, which its browser's push service gives us.
  • Technical records: our host keeps server logs (such as IP addresses and the pages requested) to run and protect the service. Rate limits are counted in the server's memory, not stored.

We don't show ads, sell your data, or use analytics or tracking tools. Fonts are served by the app itself, so no third party sees your visit.

Who can see it

  • Your partner sees your display name, your custom questions, your chat messages and reactions, your answers (only after you've both answered), whether you've seen their messages and revealed answers, and whether you're online or when you were last seen. Never your email address.
  • Us: the admin pages show account emails, names and dates. They don't show custom questions, chat or answers.
  • Chat messages are encrypted when stored, but this is not end-to-end encryption: the server holds the key so it can deliver them. Answers in Questions and Spicy Only, and custom questions, are not encrypted.
  • Push services: if you turn on notifications, they're delivered through your browser's push service (Google for Chrome and Android, Apple for Safari and iPhone, Mozilla for Firefox). Their content is encrypted so the push service can't read it, and it never includes names, messages or answers anyway.

Where it's processed

  • Render: the web service and PostgreSQL database are in Oregon, USA.
  • Email: {% if email_provider %}account emails are sent through {{ email_provider }}.{% else %}no email provider is configured, so account email delivery is disabled. Email contents are not sent or written to Render logs.{% endif %}

Render may process data outside Nigeria, where protections may differ from Nigerian law. If an email provider is enabled later, this notice and the consent information will be updated first.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and what you wrote: until you delete your account.
  • Deleting your account: it's closed immediately. After a 7-day cancellation period, it is permanently deleted, usually within a day, and always within 30 days, with your chat messages, reactions, and the custom questions we know you wrote.
  • A closed couple space (if either of you leaves or deletes their account): permanently deleted, with everything in it, 30 days after closing. Until then, each of you can download your data from it.
  • Answers in Questions and Spicy Only: deleted when either of you moves to the next question or resets the game, and when the space closes. Only the previous question's text is kept, never its answers.
  • Notifications: kept with your couple space and deleted with it, or with your account. A device's push address is deleted when you turn notifications off on it, sign out on it, or delete your account.
  • Server logs: the Render dashboard currently shows 14-day log retention for this workspace. Render controls log retention under its current plan and policies.
  • Database and backups: the PostgreSQL database is in Oregon and uses Render's free plan. It has a 1 GB limit, no backups, expires 30 days after creation, and has a 14-day period to upgrade before Render deletes it. If not upgraded, stored account and game data may be permanently lost. See Render's free-instance limits.

Your choices

  • Download your data: Profile → "Download my data" (needs your password).
  • Delete your account: Profile → "Delete my account" (needs your password). Signing in again within 7 days cancels it. Deleting your account is also how you withdraw your consents, because the app can't work without them.
  • Change your name: on your profile. Changing your email isn't possible in the app yet; contact us.
  • Questions or complaints: contact us first. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

Beta limits

  • One server handles everything. If it restarts or is redeployed, open games and chats drop and reconnect. A partner who loses signal without closing the app may show as online for a while.
  • The planned free Render web service may spin down after 15 minutes without traffic and can take about a minute to start again. The first visit after it sleeps may be slow, and live connections can drop.
  • The planned free Render database has a 1 GB limit, no backups, and expires after 30 days. It can be upgraded during the following 14 days; otherwise it is deleted. This means beta data could be permanently lost.
  • The current Render Hobby workspace includes 5 GB of bandwidth; additional usage is listed at $0.15/GB. The account owner must monitor usage to avoid unexpected charges. Render also lists 500 build minutes, 2 custom domains (additional domains at $0.25/domain), and up to 25 services for this workspace. Compute costs depend on the service plans selected.
  • Email delivery is disabled for this beta until a provider is configured. Email verification is unavailable and password recovery is unavailable; account-deletion and couple-space closure email notices are also not sent. Email verification is optional and not required to play.
  • Without a connection, the installed app opens to an offline page: playing and chatting need a connection. Nothing personal is stored on your device for offline use.
  • Features, questions and this notice may change during the beta. If this notice changes in a way that affects your consent, the app asks you to agree again.
