1. Who we are

Me & You is a private game and chat app for couples. It is run by [CHECK: owner's legal name or business name], based in Nigeria.

2. The short version

  • We keep only what the app needs to work: your account, your couple space, what you write in it, and a few technical records.
  • Chat messages are encrypted when stored. They are decrypted on our server to show them to you and your partner. This is not end-to-end encryption (see section 7).
  • We don't show ads, we don't sell or rent your data, and we don't use third-party analytics or tracking tools.
  • Our hosting and email providers store data outside Nigeria. We ask for your explicit consent to that when you sign up (see section 6).
  • You can see, correct, download and delete your data. If you delete your account, it is closed at once and permanently deleted after a 7-day cancellation period, always within 30 days.

3. What we collect, and why

Your account

  • Your email address: to sign you in, verify your account and send password resets. Your partner never sees it.
  • A password: stored only as a one-way hash. We can't read it.
  • Your display name: what your partner sees.
  • When you joined, when you last signed in, and whether and when you verified your email.
  • Your confirmation that you are 18 or older, and your consents: what you agreed to, which policy version, and when. [DEPENDS ON: pre-launch consent feature]

Your couple space

  • Which couple space you belong to, when you joined or left, and invitation links. Invitation links are stored only as a fingerprint (a hash), never in readable form, and they expire.
  • Custom questions you or your partner add, stored with which of you wrote each one. Both of you can see them. [DEPENDS ON: pre-launch: the author is recorded from launch on; older questions have no recorded author.]
  • Your comfort setting (Sweet or Spicy). [DEPENDS ON: comfort feature]

Games

  • The state of each game you play: which card is showing, your place in the deck, and whose turn it is.
  • Private answers in Questions mode are kept only for the current card, so your partner sees yours after you've both answered. They are deleted when either of you moves to the next card, resets, or the couple closes. They are never written to the game's history. [Future: a saved answer history would be encrypted, and this policy updated, before it is added.]
  • Reactions to revealed answers are deleted with the answers.

Chat

  • Your messages: encrypted when stored (section 7), with who sent them and when. You can delete your own messages for both of you; they are then removed from our database.
  • Reactions to messages (one emoji per person per message): stored with the message, not encrypted.
  • A "last read" marker, so we can show your unread count, and a "last seen" time, shown to your partner as "Last seen 5 min ago". While you are using the app, your partner can see that you're online and which part of the app you're in (for example "Playing Truth or Dare"). This is kept in memory only, not stored. The typing indicator is never stored.

What your partner can keep

  • Once you send a message, or your answer is revealed, your partner has seen it and may keep a copy (for example a screenshot). Deleting it removes it from our systems, not from their device or memory.

Technical records

  • Server logs: each request's IP address, time, the page or address requested, and the result. We use them to keep the service working and secure. Secret links (invitations, email verification, password resets) are designed so their secret part never appears in these logs. [CHECK: how long Render keeps logs on your plan.]
  • Rate limiting: to slow down attacks, we count recent requests per IP address in the server's memory for about a minute. These counts are not stored.
  • Cookies: a session cookie that keeps you signed in (for up to 2 weeks) and a security cookie that protects forms (CSRF). There are no advertising or analytics cookies.
  • Your browser also stores a note that you've seen the game guide, and the app can cache its own design files (pictures, styles, scripts) so it loads faster. Neither contains personal data.

What we don't collect

  • No birth date (only your confirmation that you are 18 or older), no phone number, no location, no contacts, no photos, voice or video. No payment details: the app is free.

5. Sensitive data

Me & You is designed for couples, and many questions, dares, answers, custom questions and chat messages may be sexual or intimate. Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) this can be sensitive personal data. We process it only because you give your explicit consent at sign-up, and only to run the app for you and your partner.

7. How we protect your data

  • All traffic uses HTTPS.
  • Passwords are stored as one-way hashes.
  • Chat messages are encrypted at rest (Fernet: AES with an integrity check). The key is kept separately from the database. If someone obtained a copy of the database or a backup, the messages would be unreadable to them.
  • This is not end-to-end encryption. Our server decrypts messages to deliver them to you and your partner. Someone with access to both our server's configuration and our database could read them. We don't read your messages.
  • Invitation and reset links are single-use or short-lived, and kept out of logs.
  • Only the two members of a couple space can see its games, answers and chat. This is checked by our server on every action, not just hidden on screen.
  • Staff access: the site's administrators can see account details (email, display name, dates) and couple membership only. They cannot see the text of custom questions, chat messages or game answers in the admin pages. (Built: commit 849f628 hides custom-question text from every admin page; chat and answers were never in the admin.)

Both of you are told by email and in the app, with the date until which you can download your data. The notice doesn't say who closed the space or why. It is permanently deleted 30 days after closing. Within those 30 days an administrator can restore it, but only if both partners ask. Either partner can ask us to delete it sooner. Deleting your account also closes your couple space, so everything in it, including your partner's messages and questions, is permanently deleted 30 days after it closes. Both of you are told by email and in the app that the space has closed, and each of you can download your own data before then. [DEPENDS ON: pre-launch account deletion]

9. Your rights

Under the NDPA 2023 and the GAID 2025 you have the right to:

  • Access your data, and download it in a portable format: use "Download my data" on your profile page. The file contains your account details, consents, your couple's custom questions (yours marked where we know), your own chat messages (decrypted) and your reactions. It doesn't include your partner's messages. If your couple space has closed, you can still download your data from it until it is deleted, 30 days after closing. [DEPENDS ON: pre-launch data export]
  • Correct your data: you can change your display name on your profile page. Changing your email address isn't possible in the app yet; contact us and we'll help.
  • Delete your account and data: use "Delete account" on your profile page. [DEPENDS ON: pre-launch account deletion]
  • Object to processing based on our legitimate interests (server logs and rate limiting). We'll stop unless we have compelling reasons to continue, such as keeping the service secure.
  • Restrict processing: ask us to pause using some of your data while a question about it is resolved, for example if you say it's inaccurate.
  • Withdraw consent: you can see your consents and their dates on your profile page. The app can't work without them, so withdrawing consent means deleting your account.
  • Complain: contact us first at {{ contact_email }}. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

We answer requests within [CHECK: e.g. 30 days].

10. Age

Me & You is only for people aged 18 or older. You must confirm this when you sign up. If we learn an account belongs to someone under 18, we will delete it.

11. Data breaches

If a breach of personal data is likely to put users' rights at risk, we report it to the NDPC within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, as the NDPA requires. If the risk to you is high, we also tell the affected users immediately, explaining what happened and what to do.

12. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes in a way that affects your consent, the app asks you to read and accept the new version before you continue. The version and date are at the top.
